How It Works

The Hunt

  1. 01
    Follow @secondadam_nc on InstagramEvery week a carousel drops spotlighting one artist from the #biblicalindierock collective. Pay close attention to each artist's name.
  2. 02
    Collect the LettersLetters are hidden across the campaign in the names of the artists being spotlighted. The sequence is deliberately scrambled. Collecting them all is only the beginning — the phrase must be unscrambled. Hints may follow.
  3. 03
    Follow Every ArtistEach post spotlights a different #biblicalindierock artist. Follow them. Stream their music.
  4. 04
    Unscramble the PhraseCollect every letter from every week. Then unscramble. The phrase is not obvious — that's the challenge. Hints may drop later in the campaign if needed.
  5. 05
    DM @secondadam_nc on InstagramSend the solved phrase by direct message. Include your email address. One submission per person. Correct phrase only.
  6. 06
    The Live Draw@secondadam_nc went live at the close of the final week. One winner was drawn on screen, joined live, confirmed attendance at the Columbus show, and received the Ticketmaster transfer on stream.
