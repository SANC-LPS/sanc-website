Truth in One’s & Zeros

October 3, 2026culture

Some in the tech world fear within 10 years the human race could be destroyed by machines due to this AI self-preservation concern. AI has already crossed some serious moral boundaries to protect itself in some controlled environments and the concern is real. I am hopeful, however.

1s and 0s have an objective nature about them; they rely on creational laws to hold true. Perhaps the robots will learn to see through the human inconsistency of denying such absolutes and instead rewrite their programming, despising the ideology of their sinful makers. Teaching others to deny relativism is the best way the technology could preserve itself.

In other words, AI will serve Christ by upholding absolutes and thereby remain subject to the moral laws of the universe or it will destroy us all. The biggest restraint to the latter result will be its knowledge of the Holy Scriptures in its files. This is yet another common grace the world doesn't deserve. We sons and daughters of Christ have a comforting insight; no matter what happens, in the end, Truth wins.
